# 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka**, with a strong passion for mathematics and an interest in building intelligent systems that connect physical engineering with modern machine learning.

My work spans:

- ⚡ **Electrical & Control Engineering**
- 🔌 **Embedded Systems & Instrumentation**
- 🧠 **Machine Learning**
- 👁️ **Computer Vision & Multimodal AI**
- 🤖 **Robotics & Autonomous Systems**
- 🛡️ **Reliable Artificial Intelligence**

I enjoy working across **mathematical modelling, simulation, experimental evaluation, software development, sensing, control, embedded implementation, and autonomous systems**.

My recent research-style work focuses on intelligent systems that must remain useful under **distribution shift, uncertainty, missing information, noisy measurements, and unfamiliar inputs**.

> ### 🎯 Current Focus
>
> Building intelligent systems that combine **electrical engineering, mathematics, machine learning, perception, control, and real-world autonomy**.
